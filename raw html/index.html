FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Still have questions?

Will the Ultra Matte Dry Touch Sunscreen leave a white cast?

No. The formula dries down to an invisible matte finish with zero white cast. Its advanced hybrid UV filters disperse evenly across the skin instead of sitting on the surface, so it blends in seamlessly across most skin tones.

Why doesn’t this sunscreen feel greasy?

Its mousse texture is designed to absorb quickly and leave a matte finish.

Can I wear it under makeup?

Yes. Apply it as the last step of your morning skincare routine.

Does Ultra Matte Dry Touch Sunscreen pill when layered with skincare?

Allow each skincare layer to absorb before applying sunscreen.

Can I wear this sunscreen while swimming or working out?

Reapply sunscreen regularly, especially after swimming or sweating.
